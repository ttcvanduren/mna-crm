Data & sources

Everything that comes into the CRM, where it came from, and backups

1 Company lists

Buyer lists and other Excel / CSV files. Columns are recognised automatically; existing companies are matched and updated, not duplicated.

2 Transactions

S&P Capital IQ, PitchBook, Mergermarket or your own Excel. Deals are merged on transaction ID or target + year; acquirers can become companies.

3 Research (Claude)

Select companies on the Companies page → ✦ Export for research, let Claude Code research the file, then import the results here (profiles, people, acquisitions, descriptions, industry & niche).

4 People & connectors

Apollo for decision makers and verified emails. Everything works without connectors.

Backup & restore

A backup holds everything: companies, projects, pitches, transactions and templates. Restoring overwrites companies with the same ID and merges the rest.

Delete

Download a backup first. In the team database this deletes for everyone.
